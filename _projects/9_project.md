## Summary

Course project in ECN-205 (Analog Circuits) on MOSFET amplifier simulation and analysis using LTSpice.

**Duration:** January 2024 -- June 2024  
**Instructor:** Prof. Sudeb Dasgupta

## Simulations

- Common-source, common-gate, and common-drain amplifier stages.
- DC operating-point and bias stability checks.
- AC gain, bandwidth, pole-zero, and impedance analysis.
- Transient response and distortion behavior.

## Design Exercises

- Multi-stage amplifier with inter-stage coupling.
- Differential amplifier with CMRR-focused analysis.
- Active-load configurations using current mirrors.

## Outcome

- Established gain-bandwidth-power trade-offs across topologies.
- Improved proficiency in LTSpice workflows and parametric sweeps.
- Built practical intuition for analog design constraints.
