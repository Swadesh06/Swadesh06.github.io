## Summary

Rural education initiative under Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA) to set up a school-level science lab in Rithaura Grunt village.

**Duration:** October 2023 -- April 2024  
**Affiliation:** UBA, IIT Roorkee  
**Role:** Project Lead

## Work Done

- Planned lab infrastructure and equipment procurement.
- Set up basic modules for physics, chemistry, and biology experiments.
- Ran orientation and practical sessions for students and teachers.
- Built a maintenance and handover process with local stakeholders.

## Impact

- Enabled practical science sessions for 200+ students.
- Trained 15+ teachers for lab operations.
- Introduced 50+ curriculum-aligned experiments.
- Extended support to nearby schools through shared usage.
