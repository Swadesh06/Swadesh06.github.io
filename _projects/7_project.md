## Summary

Case-study submission for the Switch International Energy Case Competition, focused on policy and infrastructure analysis for the Philippines and Costa Rica.

**Duration:** October 2023  
**Role:** Team Member

## Scope

- Compared national energy mixes and renewable adoption pathways.
- Evaluated grid reliability, storage readiness, and infrastructure gaps.
- Reviewed policy instruments, implementation constraints, and incentive design.
- Assessed energy security risks and resilience requirements.

## Key Findings

- Both countries have strong renewable expansion potential, but different bottlenecks.
- Policy execution gaps, not policy intent, were the main limiting factor.
- Grid modernization and storage investment are critical for scaling renewables.
- Regional cooperation and technology transfer can reduce transition risk.

## Deliverables

- Country-wise policy gap report.
- Short-term and long-term recommendations for renewable transition and energy security.
