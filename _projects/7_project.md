## Overview

A comprehensive case study conducted for the Switch International Energy Case Competition, analyzing the energy landscapes of the Philippines and Costa Rica.

## Project Details

**Duration:** October 2023  
**Competition:** Switch International Energy Case Competition  
**Focus Countries:** Philippines and Costa Rica  
**Role:** Team Member

## Objectives

Analyze current and potential future policies for energy consumption and reserves in Costa Rica and the Philippines, studying scope for policies around renewability and improved measures against scarcity of energy resources.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### Current Energy Landscape

1. **Energy Mix Assessment**
   - Fossil fuel dependency
   - Renewable energy integration
   - Nuclear and alternative energy sources
   - Import/export dynamics

2. **Infrastructure Evaluation**
   - Grid capacity and reliability
   - Distribution networks
   - Storage capabilities
   - Smart grid adoption

### Policy Analysis

1. **Existing Policies**
   - Regulatory frameworks
   - Incentive structures
   - Environmental regulations
   - International commitments

2. **Gap Identification**
   - Policy shortcomings
   - Implementation challenges
   - Resource allocation issues
   - Stakeholder engagement gaps

## Focus Areas

### Renewable Energy Transition

- **Solar Energy:** Potential for large-scale solar installations
- **Wind Power:** Geographic suitability and wind farm opportunities
- **Hydroelectric:** Sustainable water resource management
- **Geothermal:** Volcanic region exploitation (Philippines)
- **Biomass:** Agricultural waste utilization

### Energy Security

- **Supply Diversification:** Reducing dependency on single sources
- **Storage Solutions:** Battery and pumped hydro storage
- **Grid Modernization:** Smart grid technologies
- **Disaster Resilience:** Climate-proofing energy infrastructure

## Policy Recommendations

### Short-term Measures

- Enhanced renewable energy incentives
- Grid infrastructure upgrades
- Energy efficiency programs
- Public awareness campaigns

### Long-term Strategy

- Carbon neutrality roadmaps
- Regional energy cooperation
- Technology transfer initiatives
- Research and development investments

## Country-Specific Insights

### Philippines

- High potential for solar and wind energy
- Need for grid modernization in island regions
- Opportunities in geothermal expansion
- Challenges in fossil fuel phase-out

### Costa Rica

- Already high renewable energy penetration
- Focus on transport electrification
- Hydroelectric sustainability concerns
- Tourism sector energy transformation

## Impact and Findings

Our analysis highlighted significant opportunities for both countries to enhance energy security while advancing sustainability goals. Key findings included:

- Substantial untapped renewable energy potential
- Policy gaps in implementation mechanisms
- Need for enhanced regional cooperation
- Importance of technology and innovation investment
