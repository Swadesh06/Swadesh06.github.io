Hey there! I'm an undergrad at [IIT Roorkee](https://www.iitr.ac.in/) studying Electronics and Communication Engineering, but I spend most of my time thinking about Computer Vision, Diffusion Models, and how to make AI systems both powerful and safe. My research interests lie at the intersection of Generative AI, Interpretability, and adversarial robustness of Vision-Language Models.

Over the past couple of years, I've been fortunate to work on some really cool problems across different labs and companies. I've collaborated with [Virginia Tech](https://ece.vt.edu/) on interpretability in LLMs, built 3D scene generation frameworks at [AuraML](https://www.auraml.com/), optimized business workflows with [Ernst and Young](https://www.ey.com/en_in), developed multi-task RL systems at [IIIT Hyderabad's Robotics Research Centre](https://robotics.iiit.ac.in/), worked on medical AI at [IIT Bombay's Koita Centre](https://www.kcdh.iitb.ac.in/), and conducted research in collaboration with [BOSCH](https://www.bosch-ai.com/) on diffusion models. Each of these experiences has shaped how I think about AI and its real-world applications.

My work has been published at venues like TMLR and NeurIPS workshops, and I was honored to receive the Best Paper Award at MLRC 2025 for our reproducibility study on adversarial attacks in VLMs. Currently, I'm deep into research on geometric manipulation of latent manifolds in Diffusion Models and building better frameworks for understanding what's happening inside generative vision models.

Beyond research, I'm actively involved in the [Data Science Group at IIT Roorkee](https://dsgiitr.github.io/dsg-website/) as Joint Secretary, where we organize workshops, hackathons, and publish research at top-tier conferences with teams of undergrads. I also serve as a reviewer for TMLR and lead editorial efforts for the Eco Group, working on sustainability initiatives on campus.

When I'm not debugging code or reading papers, you'll probably find me at campus chai spots discussing the latest ML papers, or exploring Roorkee's food scene. Feel free to reach out if you want to chat about research, collaborate on projects, or just grab a cup of chai!
