Village Science Lab Setup

Science education initiative for rural development

Overview

A rural development initiative conducted under Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA) to establish a science laboratory in Rithaura Grunt village, providing rural children access to practical scientific education.

Project Details

Duration: October 2023 – April 2024
Affiliation: Unnat Bharat Abhiyan, IIT Roorkee
Location: Rithaura Grunt Village
Role: Team Member

Mission

Provide rural children access to practical applications and experiments of topics in their studies, facilitating a practice of practical exploration along with theoretical concepts.

Objectives

  1. Infrastructure Development
    • Establish fully-equipped science laboratory
    • Procure experimental equipment and materials
    • Create safe learning environment
    • Develop sustainable maintenance model
  2. Educational Impact
    • Bridge theory-practice gap in rural education
    • Enhance science learning outcomes
    • Inspire scientific curiosity
    • Improve academic performance

Project Components

Laboratory Setup

  • Physics Equipment: Basic mechanics, optics, and electricity experiments
  • Chemistry Lab: Safe chemical handling setup with basic reagents
  • Biology Station: Microscopes and specimen preparation facilities
  • Mathematics Corner: Geometric models and calculation aids

Experimental Modules

Designed hands-on experiments aligned with school curriculum:

  • Class 6-8: Basic physics principles, simple chemistry reactions
  • Class 9-10: Advanced experiments in mechanics, electricity, chemical reactions
  • Class 11-12: Complex experimental setups for board exam preparation

Implementation Phases

Phase 1: Planning and Design (Oct 2023)

  • Village needs assessment
  • Infrastructure evaluation
  • Budget planning
  • Equipment procurement list

Phase 2: Setup and Installation (Nov 2023 - Jan 2024)

  • Laboratory construction
  • Equipment installation
  • Safety measures implementation
  • Inventory management system

Phase 3: Training and Launch (Feb - Mar 2024)

  • Teacher training programs
  • Student orientation sessions
  • Safety protocol training
  • Experimental demonstration sessions

Phase 4: Monitoring and Support (Apr 2024)

  • Regular visits and support
  • Maintenance training
  • Performance evaluation
  • Sustainability planning

Key Activities

  • Science Exhibition: Organized village-wide science fair
  • Demonstration Sessions: Regular hands-on experiment sessions
  • Teacher Training: Capacity building for local teachers
  • Student Workshops: Interactive learning sessions

Challenges and Solutions

Challenges Faced

  • Limited infrastructure in rural setting
  • Resource constraints
  • Teacher training requirements
  • Sustainability concerns

Solutions Implemented

  • Modular, low-cost equipment solutions
  • Community involvement in maintenance
  • Comprehensive training programs
  • Local partnership development

Impact

Quantitative Impact

  • Laboratory serving 200+ students
  • 15+ teachers trained
  • 50+ experiments made accessible
  • Regular usage by 5 schools in vicinity

Qualitative Impact

  • Increased student engagement in science
  • Improved understanding of scientific concepts
  • Enhanced interest in STEM careers
  • Community pride in educational facility

Community Engagement

  • Parent involvement in lab activities
  • Village elder support for sustainability
  • Local artisan engagement for equipment maintenance
  • Integration with existing educational initiatives

Sustainability Model

  • Local management committee formation
  • Revenue generation through science camps
  • Partnership with nearby schools
  • IIT Roorkee ongoing mentorship

Learning Outcomes

  • Rural development project management
  • Community engagement strategies
  • Educational program design
  • Sustainable development practices
  • Cross-cultural collaboration

This project represents IIT Roorkee’s commitment to social responsibility and educational equity, bringing quality science education to underserved rural communities.
